Social Layer Check

200 simulated campaigns of 10 weeks: five ways of handling the table, each with five different Session Zeros. The gate asks two things: can every conflict be settled more than one way, and does every level-3 subclass pick fit the player who made it?

How it was run

Simulated DMs. Ignores it: never messages anyone about it, lets every conflict card ride; Talks it out: answers every message kindly, then fixes it in DMs and group posts; Says it straight: tells Jess straight, calls a vote on the rules, runs a tone check; Fixes it at the table: no DMs; passes the spotlight, calls votes and protects quiet moments during game night; Plans around it: promises to look rulings up (and does), plans weeks with a serious beat and a goofy one. All of them prep the same way (the balanced prep style from the M2 harness) and plant a seed most weeks.

Session Zeros. A careful one (compromise tone, every ask agreed, the X-card, quick rulings looked up after), three that each lean into one conflict, and a careless one (pushed back on every ask, no X-card, rule of cool).

Subclass picks. 60 parties with no seeds, then 60 where one player's nudging seed was planted in week 1 and paid off before the level-up. Every pick also has to have a line for that player to say at the table.

Reproduce. lua tools/sim/social.lua from the project root (about five minutes), then python3 tools/sim/social_report.py for this page.
