Rules engine check

The headless SRD 5.2.1 engine runs the slice party (Fighter, Rogue, Cleric and Wizard at levels 2 to 4, all twelve subclasses) through thousands of fights against the slice's monsters. This page checks the M3 gate: hand-checked combat cases match the rules, and the difficulty meter predicts outcomes.

    Fine print

    What the engine simplifies

    Grid and space. A 5-foot grid with diagonals counting 5 feet. Every creature takes one square, even the Large ogre and dire wolf. Open ground: no cover, no obstacles, everyone sees everyone unless a condition says otherwise.

    Tactics. Players and monsters follow simple, readable tactics (focus the closest threat, heal the fallen, use area spells on crowds). Real players do better and worse in different places, so treat the numbers as a baseline, not a promise.

    Cozy defaults. Monsters don't attack characters who are already down. Deaths come from failed Death Saving Throws and massive damage. Each character carries one Potion of Healing; the party starts each fight with full resources after casting its 8-hour buffs (Mage Armor and Aid).

    Not modelled yet. Hiding, grappling and shoving, ready actions, flying, and non-combat spells. Those arrive with game night at the table in M4.
