BEHIND THE SCREEN - playtest build
==================================

Thank you for playing! You're the Dungeon Master for four friends who meet once a
week to play a fantasy tabletop game. You plan each session, then watch it play out
at the table and make the calls when a card slides across your screen. Between
sessions you read the group chat, smooth over arguments and keep everyone coming
back. You don't need to know D&D, and there's no way to "lose".

This build is Session Zero plus the first four sessions of a campaign.
All told it takes about 90 minutes. Play it in one go, or stop after any session:
the game saves when each session ends, and "Continue" on the title card picks up
with the next one. (If you quit in the middle of a session, that session starts over.)


STARTING
--------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (your Desktop is fine). Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Open "BehindtheScreen.exe" (in the "Behind the Screen" folder).
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because this test build isn't signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. A short note says what this prototype is (and isn't). Click "Agree" to start.

It needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. If the window is bigger than your screen, double-click
its title bar to maximize it: the game scales to fit any size.


CONTROLS
--------
Almost everything is the mouse. A few keys help:
  Enter            the next step (run game night, see the report, next session)
  N                your notes (the Notes button)      S   the Story log
  Esc              close your notes, a sheet, a conversation or a pop-up
  M                music on / off
On game night:
  Space            skip ahead
  F / G            faster / slower
  Tab              the night's log
  H                your own rolls: behind your screen or in the open
  T                a gentle timer on each call
  C                a photo of the table
  1 to 6           pick a card's option
  Right mouse drag look around    Mouse wheel  zoom    R  reset the camera


WHAT WE'D LIKE FROM YOU
-----------------------
- Play it the way you'd naturally play. There are no wrong choices; we want to
  see what you do, not whether you're good at it.
- After each session the game asks two quick questions. A click each.
- After the fourth session there's a short survey, with two optional typed answers.
  Honest is more useful than nice.
- Then send us your playtest log (see below). That's it!


SENDING YOUR LOG
----------------
Double-click "Open my playtest log folder.bat" in this folder. (Or press Windows+R,
type  %APPDATA%\behind_the_screen  and press Enter.)

Send us the file  playtest_log.jsonl  from that folder. If the game ever showed a
"Something broke" box, please send  error_log.txt  too.

What's in the log: the choices you made in the game, the game's own numbers for each
session, how long prep and game night took, and your survey answers. Nothing about
you or your computer. You can open it in Notepad to see for yourself.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Something broke" on screen: the game wrote what happened to error_log.txt (see
  above). Close the game and start it again; "Continue" takes you back to the start
  of the session you were on (or the next one, if the session had already ended).
- It won't start at all: tell us your Windows version and what you saw.

Thanks again. Every log helps.
