# Behind the Screen v2: The Museum of Nightmares

October 30, 2026. The full design, with the feedback table, the room-by-room plan and the open questions, is
the living doc "Behind the Screen v2: The Museum of Nightmares" (https://claude.ai/artifact/QXuhtYeFWy6rqiTXLimQbj).
This file is the short version that lives with the code.

## Why

The Discord feedback (Straps, Hotsoop, Tasia via Straps) says the table works and the story doesn't: random
hands of story cards made stories that didn't fit together, and nothing visibly remembered what the table did.
v2 keeps the four friends, Session Zero, the rules engine and game night, and replaces the story deck, factions,
seeds, the valley and the planner with a real published adventure, cut into cards that remember.

## The prologue

*The Museum of Nightmares* by Luke Russell (DMs Guild), as a one-shot prologue that takes as many sessions as the
table needs (three to five in testing). Strictly the book: nothing from the Silent Bells shows while it runs. The
main campaign after it will be the next adventure the user uploads, so nothing in the format is museum-specific.

## The loop

1. **Session Zero, then why each hero came.** Two of the book's background hooks per player, one they lean
   toward. Each hook pays off in one room.
2. **Prep on the museum map.** Rooms to ready within the week's 10 hours (an unprepped room plays worse), and the
   book's own advice as knobs (how to run a room; who would love or hate it). Euryale's card is on the board.
3. **Game night room by room.** The book's moments as calls, forks the table picks by what they want, and a cut
   upstairs to Euryale's thoughts when her card gains a mark.
4. **The report**, with what each room left behind.
5. **Between sessions:** a player request answered four ways (yes / with a laugh / no, with a reason / yes, but
   it's dangerous), loose ends that re-home a card the party skipped, favors with one help and one hurt.
6. **Boss night:** a showdown board (how she opens, how far she goes), a talk round, the fight, five endings,
   then the journey recap (route, marks, each hero's history in short lines, what each friend wants next).

## The ideas the feedback asked for

- **Living cards.** Thirteen marks on Euryale's card, each from something the party did, each changing who fights
  beside her, what the party brings, or how easily she talks (`game/data/museum/module.lua`, `M.marks`).
- **Harsh calls that feel earned.** Chances taken knowingly bank a fair warning; a harsh call spends one. Without
  one in the bank it costs trust and adds suspicion. A *Dark and dangerous* table takes the first one each night
  free (`adventure.free_harsh`, `museum_night.harsh`).
- **Prompted adjustments.** Loose ends ("where does the Knight of Shades turn up?") and favors ("one way it helps,
  one way it hurts").
- **An inner voice.** Euryale's interludes upstairs.

## Where it lives

See `AGENTS.md` ("This branch") for the file table and the checks. In short: the adventure is data in
`game/data/museum/`; `game/model/adventure.lua` enters it and keeps its state in `campaign.adv`;
`museum_week.lua` and `museum_night.lua` run the week and the night; `game/rules/museum.lua` holds the book's
monsters; the screens are `game/ui/museum_*.lua`.

## How it tested (October 30)

Night scores are the table's average fun out of 100.

| DM (12 seeds each) | Sessions | Endings | Build-up nights | Boss night |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Careful | 3 (one run 4) | 8 escape, 4 fallen | 77 | 60 |
| Random | 3 to 5 | 6 fallen, 4 bargain, 2 escape | 60 | 58 |
| Careless | 3 to 5 | 6 fallen, 3 bargain, 3 escape | 42 | 58 |

- The real engine plays the whole prologue (`MODES=8 TIMEOUT=2500 tools/tests/engine_check.sh out`). Its first
  run caught a build that left out the museum's night (a module reached only through `util.lazy`), now caught by
  `tools/tests/require_check.py`.
- The night scores caught two pacing holes: a session of just the stairs (scored 8) and a one-scene boss night
  (35). The stairs now fold into boss night (`adventure.boss_week`), a night never opens by going straight up
  them, the floor's last room is finished rather than stranded (`museum_night.FINISH_FLOOR`), and boss night
  ends with Flitwidget's thanks as a scene (`museum_night.thanks`).
- 178 game-night fights in the sweep, 3 lost (random and careless DMs); a lost boss fight sends the party home
  to rest. The fights may be soft; the playtest decides.
- "Her pack" (huntlings that ran) reaches the boss in almost every run; the "ally" ending never came up with bots.
