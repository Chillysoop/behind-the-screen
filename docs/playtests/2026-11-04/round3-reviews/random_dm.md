Playtest report: the eight random-DM prologues, files 31 to 38 (`prologue_3N_random.txt`)

I read all eight files from start to end, as a player would. I didn't open any code. Quotes are exact, with the file number and week (for example "34 W1"). I found no blockers. A random DM does break a lot of the game's promises, and the same breaks come up again and again.

## TRUE: untrue or contradictory

**T1. The lock card names the wrong hero.** Bad (true/fits). Seen in 3 runs.
- 31 W1: "[dm] Pip cracks her knuckles and kneels by the case." / ">> Picks out: Thessaly has the picks out" / "You give Thessaly a look over the screen. She puts the picks away, sulking."
- 38 W1: "Pip cracks her knuckles…" / ">> Picks out: Brannoc has the picks out". Brannoc then rolls Sleight of Hand. The chain says "Pip went for the donation box's lock, so Brannoc picked the lock".
- 33 W1: Jess proposed the lock, but Thessaly the wizard kneels and holds the picks.
- Right: the hero who kneels is the one on the card and the roll, and by default that's whoever proposed it (Pip).

**T2. A destroyed Sentinel still watches them leave.** Bad (true). 2 of the 2 runs that fought it.
- 34 W1 and 38 W1: "[good] That's the Sentinel done." then "[good] They leave the case alone and head for the far doors. The metal guard watches them all the way."
- Right: give the fought branch its own line.

**T3. The recap says they "stilled the Bone Construct" when they smashed it.** Bad (true). 5 of the 5 runs that fought it: 32 W3, 34 W2, 35 W2, 36 W2, 37 W2.
- 34 W2 is the worst: "fought their way past the Sentinel…, stilled the Bone Construct". In that run the ritual had failed and it came to blades.

**T4. The DM's Telpur voice is credited to a hero.** Bad (true). 3 of 3 runs.
- 32 W2: "Oswin did Telpur's voice, all of it." 35 W2: "Pip did…". 38 W2: "Thessaly did…".
- The table heard "[dm] The voice is terrible. Jess cries laughing."

**T5. The chain gives Francs to the wrong hero, for the wrong cause, or counts them twice.** Bad (true; the "so" isn't a real cause).
- Douse: 32 W1 "They doused the hedges with pond water, so Thessaly found a Flitwidget Franc." Pip found it, on a Perception triumph. 34 W1 says the same, but Brannoc found it. 38 W1 says "so Oswin found a Flitwidget Franc", and no Franc was ever heard.
- Pond: 33 W1 "Pip fished a little brass key out of the pond, so Pip found a Flitwidget Franc." That Franc came from the earlier triumph.
- Coin: 35, 36 and 37 say "They dropped a coin in the donation box, so Oswin found a Flitwidget Franc." The table only heard "Something warm settles over Oswin."
- Double counts: in 31 W2 the duellist Franc is listed twice. In 36 W3 the chain lists 3 Francs for Thessaly when the table heard 2.
- The Franc pools then jump. In 31 W1 the pool says 2 but only 1 was heard. In 33 W1 it says 4 and 2 were heard.

**T6. Praise goes to the wrong person.** Bad (true).
- 33 W1: "Brannoc's plan, Thessaly's timing: it works perfectly". Dev had argued "skip the homework and fight it", and the ritual was Mara and Sam's idea.
- 34 W1 and 38 W1: "Thessaly and Brannoc pulled off a move together in that fight that neither could have alone." Neither fight log shows anything joint. In 38 Brannoc missed both swings.

**T7. Flitwidget's memory of the draught is the wrong way round.** Bad (true/alive).
- In 32 Pip was caught ("Put it back, and we'll say no more about it"), yet at the end he says "My museum's back. My draught isn't."
- In 31, 33 and 34 Pip really did steal it, and he never mentions it.

**T8. "Took the breakages out of their reward" changes nothing.** Bad (true).
- 34 W4: "I've taken the breakages out of it. Only fair." The reward is still 40 each, the same as 36, which had no breakages.
- 38 W3: same line, and the reward is 20 each, the same as the 35 and 37 escapes.
- 32 W4: "Minus a garden. And a guardian." In 32 the garden was only doused, not burned.
- Right: show the deduction in the number, or drop the line.

**T9. "They'd been warned" when nobody warned them.** Bad (true). 2 times.
- 32 W3 and 34 W3: Pip slips upstairs alone, the DM picks "Let it cost them: Euryale is waiting.", and the table hears "It stings, but nobody argues. They'd been warned."
- The line is correct in 32 W4 and on the 34 Mech switch, where the DM did describe the danger.

**T10. The DM's targeting choice is ignored.** Bad (true). 3 times.
- 32 W2: "*2. Go after Thessaly, who has the fewest hit points." Then "[dev] Oof. Right for the weak spot." and every mace swing goes to Brannoc.
- 36 W2: "*1. Go for Thessaly…" The boss flees on its turn instead.
- 37 W2: "The Lecturer can't take its eyes off Oswin… it's Oswin it wants." Its Bored to Death then targets Brannoc twice.

**T11. "How do you want to do this?" goes to Dev, then Telpur lands the blow.** Bad (true). 32 W3: "The Lecturer is about to fall to Brannoc" / "Dev, how do you want to do this?". Telpur's Pointy Toe kills it, and Dev never describes anything.

**T12. The reward talk contradicts itself.** Bad.
- 31 W3: Oswin "believes it" when Flitwidget says "Reward? I'm sure I said biscuits.", and Jess says "Oh! Well, that's that, then." Her next line is "So. The reward. Thirty gold each seems low, for a Gorgon."
- After the public 40-gold handshake: "[twist] Pip counts the loot alone and reports 'about thirty coppers, give or take.'"
- That loot twist appears 6 times across the runs (Brannoc does it in 37 W2), and every time there's no loot to count.

**T13. The opening near miss contradicts itself.** Minor. 31 W1 and 35 W1: "They're going in half blind." / "Not quite, but they come away knowing more than they did." / "They take the job knowing only what the brochure says."

**T14. The scarabs "go to her" after being killed.** Minor. In 5 of the 5 runs that fought the chamber, the swarm dies, then "Its beetles are mine now." / "So its scarabs crawled up through the walls to her." A swarm then shows up in her lair. Say these are more beetles from the walls.

**T15. Smaller slips.**
- 34 W1: the hedge cat that "twists, sprouts and roots itself" is later "only ever a hedge."
- 31 W2 and 33 W2: one huntling flees, then "The last huntlings scatter into the dioramas".
- Recaps that just repeat themselves: "Euryale got away, so Euryale slipped away." (33 W3, 37 W4, 38 W3).
- 35 W3 recap: "held off the huntlings, just" after the whole party went down.
- 31 W1, at the first rest of the prologue: "Nobody had Hit Dice left to spend".

## TRUE/ALIVE: promises the game doesn't keep

**P1. The vorpal sword.** Bad.
- When the DM answered "yes" (32, 33, 34, 35, 37), the sword never appears.
- When the DM answered "danger" (31), it does appear, and in the wrong room. In the gift shop scene: "And behind Euryale's couch, right where you said it would be: a vorpal sword." They had bargained and never searched the lair.

**P2. Flitwidget's bracelet is never handed over.** Bad. 35, 36, 37.
- The only hint is Euryale's interlude: "The gnome gave them a trinket."
- Then in the lair: "Flitwidget's bracelet is on a wrist and ready". Whose wrist? Nobody saw him give it.

**P3. Petrification and the mirror payoff happen in silence.** Bad.
- 32 W4: the log shows Oswin "Turning to stone" and Euryale "Meets her reflection… Turning to stone". The table hears nothing until "Oswin cracks out of the stone, gasping."
- 35 W3: the same thing happens to Pip and Thessaly.
- The cupboard-door mirror they carried for a week pays off and nobody sees it.
- Brannoc being Charmed in 32 W4 is also silent. It is narrated well in 36 and 38.

**P4. Session Zero agreements that were never made.** Bad (true).
- Session Zero is introductions only. Even so, every run has "You keep the creepy-crawlies off screen, as you agreed with Sam at Session Zero."
- 34, 35, 36 and 37 add "You leave the gore out, as you promised Mara at Session Zero." 34 has "'Remember what we said at Session Zero?' A couple of nods." twice.
- "Off screen" is then broken anyway: 35 "[sam] Twenty. On the Giant Scarab Swarm. Huh."

**P5. Stolen and bought items don't match.** Bad.
- Pip steals "a quiver of Telpur's fire arrows", but the fights apply "Telpur's slime, +2 acid". Nobody stole or bought slime in 31, and in 35 the slime shows up a scene before they buy it.
- No fight log ever shows fire damage.
- In 35 and 36 Telpur sells them the same quiver Pip just stole, and nobody remarks on it.
- 36 W2 says the same effect twice: "Out come Telpur's fire arrows and the bucket of slime… Pip hands round the fire arrows nobody paid for: every hero's first weapon hit each turn burns".

**P6. Things set up and never paid off.** Minor.
- The red switch "saved for the Gorgon" is never flipped (35, 37).
- "Lets X have first pick of whatever the party finds next" appears 6 times with no follow-up. In 32 W4 and 35 W3 it lands right as Euryale yields.
- 37 W3: the hint "you never did look properly at the garden" leads to nothing, and the trip doesn't mention the garden was burned.
- Exhibit B never appears in 33, although the DM gave the same answer as in the runs where he does.
- Pip meets Euryale's eyes alone (32, 34) at no cost.
- Splitting the party ("cut back and forth like a heist movie", "Thessaly's way wins, narrowly") changes nothing.

## Five things that would most improve the game
1. **Take the actor in every beat from the one record.** That covers the lock-card hero, the Telpur voice, Franc finders and causes, praise lines, "stilled" in recaps, and the destroyed Sentinel watching them. Findings T1 to T6.
2. **Keep the game's promises, with visible consequences.** The vorpal "yes", the bracelet handover, breakages that change the number, the draught memory, Session Zero agreements that are actually made, the red switch offered in the lair, and "first pick". Findings P1, P2, P4, P6, T7, T8.
3. **Say the big combat beats out loud and honour the DM's picks.** Petrify and the mirror reflection, the charm, the chosen targets, and who lands the killing blow. Findings P3, T10, T11.
4. **Make friends' lines match their vote and the room.** No same-breath reversals, no loser lines from winners, show the silent fourth vote, and keep quips specific to the room. Findings F1 to F4.
5. **Respect the state of the night.** No browsing after a total defeat or after "they back out", a real cost or comment for running past midnight, and Flitwidget kept downstairs. Findings F5 to F7.
