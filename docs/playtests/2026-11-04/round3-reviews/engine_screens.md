## The five things that would most improve the game
1. Make the boss reward follow the board: 15 each when she gets away, in one named currency (8_49 against 8_54).
2. Keep minis on their bases and visible: no heroes off the board (8_36), no stacking behind each other or walls (8_44, 8_51), and no title card over the monster (8_36). Give Euryale a front spot on boss night.
3. Declutter game night: cap and fade the floating combat labels (8_51), limit bubbles and roll cards (8_44), one side panel at a time (8_12), cards that don't cover the caption (8_23), and tips that leave (8_14, 8_17).
4. Stop cutting off the lines that decide things: the friends' game-night forecast, the private-message question (8_30), the boss options and what the party brings (8_49), and the room cards. Fix the quote and bar overlap on the end-of-night screens.
5. Make each report's chain cover every room of the night, with titles to match, and fold the repeated Franc finds into one line (8_17, 8_29, 8_39).
