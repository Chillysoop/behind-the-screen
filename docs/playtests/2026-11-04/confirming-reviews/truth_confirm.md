## Headline

The fixes landed. Most of last round's big truth breaks are gone: the lock hero rolls and gets the coins, friendly fire is heard, slime is only thrown when bought, kills and early knockouts go to the hero who struck, enemies go after the target the card picked, the destroyed Sentinel lies still, Telpur spots his own stock, the mirror and HYPER DESTROYER MODE play out loud, the bracelet and marbles are seen handed over, recaps lead with deaths and losses, and every run ends on one reward figure that adds up.

In all three runs nobody dead acts in a fight, the recap leads with the death, and the night mostly goes quiet afterwards (the exceptions are under B17). 57r is the best death handling I've seen in these logs.

What's left, in order of weight:
- The world sometimes uses something the party never did or owned. One case is a blocker: a Steam Mech fights for a party that never woke it (57c W5).

- Vote asides still count voices that never spoke.

Counts for this round (new findings plus old ones still there): blocker 1, bad 7, minor 38, polish 7.
